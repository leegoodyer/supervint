### Short description (1 line, ~150 chars)
Supervint is a free Chrome extension that alerts you the instant a new Vinted
listing matches your saved search — no auto-buy, no login, no ban risk.

### Long description (~300 words)
Supervint watches your Vinted searches in real time and pings you the moment a
matching item is listed, so you can grab the deals that sell within minutes —
before anyone else sees them.

How it works:
- Set a search for anything — a brand, a size, a price range, a rare set.
- Supervint checks for new listings on a safe, human-like schedule and alerts
  you the second a match appears.
- Click the alert and you're on the listing, ready to buy.

Why Supervint over other tools:
- **Alert-only.** It never auto-buys and never logs into your Vinted account,
  so there's no ban risk.
- **Free to start.** A free tier covers casual use; paid plans unlock more
  searches and a crowd-sourced sold-price history.
- **Built for the UK.** Sized, priced and tuned for UK Vinted buyers.

Supervint also runs a sold-price database built from real completed sales, so
you can see what items actually sold for — not what sellers are asking.
